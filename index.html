Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Nirvana Hospital in Badvel.

Where is Nirvana Hospital located and how easy is it to reach? ▼

Nirvana Hospital is situated right on Siddavatam Road, beside Kiran Hospital and directly next to the Badvel RTC Bus Stand in Sumitra Nagar. It is less than a 2-minute walk from the main bus station, making it remarkably convenient for patients traveling from rural Kadapa, Porumamilla, Mydukur, and surrounding villages.

Who is the lead surgeon and what are his qualifications? ▼

The surgical department is spearheaded by Dr. Mudamala Vishnuvardhan Reddy, MBBS, MS (General Surgery - Bangalore), FMAS, DMAS (Minimal Access Surgery - New Delhi) with training on the da Vinci Si Robotic Surgical System console. He is an alumnus of Apollo Hospitals Bangalore and RIMS Hospital Kadapa (Reg No: 100892).

Are emergency casualty and pharmacy services open at night? ▼

Yes, 100%. Nirvana Hospital operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our casualty unit, emergency resuscitation beds, doctor on call, and in-house pharmacy are staffed round the clock. You can call +91 94406 49191 anytime for ambulance support or emergency intake.

Do you accept health insurance and government medical claims? ▼

Yes, our hospital desk helps patients with documentation for private health insurance reimbursement and mediclaim policies. We provide transparent itemized discharge summaries and bills to ensure hassle-free claims.
